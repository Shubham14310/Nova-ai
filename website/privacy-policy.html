Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Nova AI (“we,” “our,” or “us”) provides this website and information about our Android application. This page explains what information may be collected when you visit this website and how it is used.

Information you provide

If you send feedback through our website, you may provide your name (optional), feedback category, and the message you write. The form sends this information to our support inbox using FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information in feedback.

Advertising and cookies

This website may display Google AdSense advertisements, subject to approval and activation. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize ads as permitted by applicable settings and law. You can manage ad personalization through Google’s Ads Settings and your browser’s cookie settings. Learn more in Google’s advertising technologies information.

Download statistics and third-party services

The website retrieves public APK download counts from the GitHub Releases API. When you follow external links or download the app, those services may process information under their own privacy policies. Our website also uses Google’s AdSense script when advertising is configured.

How we use information

  • To receive and respond to feedback or support requests.
  • To maintain and improve the website and application.
  • To display and measure advertising, where enabled.
  • To help protect the website against misuse.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information submitted through the website feedback form. Information may be processed by service providers needed to deliver feedback, advertising, or website features. Feedback messages may remain in the support inbox for as long as needed to respond and maintain support records.

Children’s privacy

Nova AI’s website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in ways that require parental consent. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit feedback, manage cookies in your browser, and adjust Google ad personalization settings. To ask about information submitted through the feedback form, contact us using the details on our Contact Us page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or its services change. The latest update date will be shown at the top of this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, please visit our Contact Us page.
