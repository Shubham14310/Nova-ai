About Nova AI

Your intelligent assistant for chat, learning, images, PDFs, and voice.

Nova AI is an AI assistant project designed to make helpful AI tools easier to access on Android. The website provides information about the app, its features, and official download instructions.

What Nova AI offers

  • AI chat for questions, ideas, and learning.
  • Image questions and AI image creation or editing features, when available.
  • PDF assistance and document questions, where supported.
  • Voice tools and read-aloud features.
  • Saved conversations and account features, where available.

Our aim

Our aim is to build a clear, accessible experience and improve Nova AI based on user feedback. Feature availability can depend on app version, service providers, and usage limits.

Feedback

If you encounter an issue or have a suggestion, use the feedback form or visit our Contact Us page.
